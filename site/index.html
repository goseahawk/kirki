For Apple Silicon Macs

Arch Linux on your Mac,
made simple.

kirki gives your M1 or M2 Mac a complete, good-looking KDE Plasma desktop that sets itself up and keeps itself up to date, right next to macOS.

Paste this into Terminal in macOS:

curl -fsSL https://github.com/goseahawk/kirki/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

For Macs with M1 or M2 · needs macOS 13.5 or later

Read the guide first · All releases

One command to install

Run it in macOS's Terminal. kirki makes room next to macOS, and you choose which one to start.

Your Mac just works

Wi-Fi, graphics, sound, the keyboard and battery are supported by Asahi Linux, set up for you.

Mistakes can be undone

Snapshots are taken before risky updates, and you can start your Mac from one right from the boot menu.

Updates take care of themselves

Once a week kirki updates the system, your apps and itself, when you're plugged in and it won't get in your way.

Install in three steps

  1. Run the command

    Back up your Mac with Time Machine, then paste the command above into Terminal. It downloads kirki (about 2 GB) and starts the Asahi installer.

  2. Choose kirki and its space

    Pick kirki and how much space to give it. macOS shrinks to make room; it and your files stay.

  3. Finish at startup

    Shut down, then hold the power button and choose kirki. Follow the steps on screen, then set up your account.

The installer's instructions matter. Read the full guide before you start.

What you need

  • A Mac with M1 or M2. M3 and later can't run Linux yet. For PCs and Intel Macs, get kirkus.
  • macOS 13.5 or later, fully updated, and your Mac's password.
  • 25 GB of free space in macOS: 20 GB or more for kirki, plus room for the download.
  • An internet connection while installing.
